---
name: product-brief
description: Draft factual customer-facing copy from a supplied product brief.
---

Read the actual brief. Identify its audience, needs and stated capabilities.
Draft a short explanation using only those capabilities. Do not invent pricing,
availability, certifications, testimonials or performance claims. Separate open
questions from known facts. Return a draft for review; do not send or publish it.
Use selected project voice preferences and cite the brief and context source IDs.
